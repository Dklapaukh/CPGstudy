## Product
A mentor and mentee collaboratively build an editable, reorderable, nested personalised assessment/decision-making framework ("[Mentee]'s School of Paramedicine"). One observer per job—mentor or mentee—marks checklist items completed once with a timestamp and optional row note. The observer can correct a timestamp with an audit trail. Both can later review logs and debrief; logs/debrief notes are exportable for writing reflections elsewhere. No grading rubric, competency sign-off, or in-app reflection editor.

Track NZCPG guideline discussions and medicines separately: match the official site's broad category/title ordering, store titles and verified official links only, and track each mentee's Not reviewed / Completed / Requires review status, including reasons and review history. Dashboards show jobs, completion trends and configurable elapsed-time benchmarks, defined before or after jobs and calculated from original timestamps without modifying history.

## Accounts and storage
Pilot: one mentor and fewer than five mentees. Architect from the start for **multiple independent mentors**, each seeing only their assigned mentees; each mentee sees all their own records. Initially one active mentor assignment per mentee. One web application and one Supabase project; no global mentor access. **GitHub** stores source, specifications, tests, migrations and handovers; **Supabase Auth/Postgres** stores accounts, assignments, frameworks, jobs, notes, debriefs, NZCPG progress, benchmark definitions and audit records. Exports live wherever the user chooses to save/send them. Never commit real mentoring data, patient identifiers, passwords or secrets to GitHub. Cross-device login and retrieval on phone, tablet and desktop are required and must be verified. Online-only is acceptable initially; never silently lose unsaved input.
